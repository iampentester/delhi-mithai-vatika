About Us

Welcome to Delhi Mithai Vatika, a well-known pure vegetarian sweet shop located at the heart of Lal Darwaza Cross Roads, Charminar.

We are known for fresh, hygienic mithai and delicious chaat. Families from Lal Darwaza, Shalibanda, Gowlipura and nearby areas visit us regularly for quality sweets at reasonable prices.

Open daily from early morning till late evening. Perfect for daily mithai, festival orders, and evening chaat.
