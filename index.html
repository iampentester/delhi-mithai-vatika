About Delhi Mithai Vatika

Located at the bustling Lal Darwaza Cross Roads near Charminar, Delhi Mithai Vatika is a beloved pure vegetarian sweet shop serving the Old City of Hyderabad.

We are known for fresh mithai, traditional flavours, and hygienic preparation. Whether you need daily sweets, festival gift boxes, or evening chaat — we welcome you with open arms.
